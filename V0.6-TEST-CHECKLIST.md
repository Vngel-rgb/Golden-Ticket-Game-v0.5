# Golden Ticket Game v0.5 mechanics playtest checklist

Use a new room after deploying this version. Existing in-progress rooms can contain older state.

## Setup and turn flow

- Host and join with 2–5 players.
- Confirm character uniqueness, readiness, starting money, six-card deal, and opening discards.
- Confirm movement wraps from space 9 to space 1.
- Confirm refill, action points, turn changes, and deck recycling.

## Board spaces

- Space 1: pay $14 and gain up to 6 remaining bars.
- Space 2: pay $3 and gain up to 2 remaining bars.
- Space 3: sweep 2 Mystery cards and gain up to 2 bars.
- Space 4: sweep 3 Sweet cards and gain up to 2 bars.
- Space 5: draw 3 and discard 2.
- Space 6: pay $5 and draw 3.
- Space 7: gain $4.
- Space 8: refill money to the character allowance.
- Space 9: sweep 1–4 Rowdy cards and gain one bar per card.

## Cards and characters

- Test every Sweet, Rowdy, and Mystery card at least once.
- Test multi-copy effects, target selection, steals, forced discards, reactions, movement, and bar returns.
- Verify Augustus, Charlie, Mike, Veruca, and Violet abilities.
- Verify Willy Wonka rewards once per encounter and moves after the encounter turn.

## End of this playtest stage

- Claim the final Wonka Bar.
- Confirm further gameplay locks.
- Confirm Golden Tickets remain hidden; the reveal and chocolate-opening presentation is intentionally reserved for the next release stage.

## Deployment

- Netlify build command: `npm run build`
- Netlify publish directory: `dist`
- Keep `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` and `VITE_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`.
- No Supabase schema reset or migration is required.
