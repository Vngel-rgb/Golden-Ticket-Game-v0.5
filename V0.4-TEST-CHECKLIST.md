# V0.4 Quick Test Checklist

- Game log begins collapsed on desktop and mobile.
- Draw and Discard boxes show only their labels and counters.
- Remaining Wonka Bars counter appears below the board with a pink border.
- Discarding a hand card moves the player exactly one numbered space, including 9 to 1.
- Landing after a discard displays a Yes/No space-effect prompt.
- AP never displays below zero.
- At zero AP, normal Play, Move, and Refill controls are unavailable; End Turn remains available.
- Sweep controls only activate on matching Sweep spaces with enough matching cards.
- Pre-match readiness notice is not carried into an active match.
