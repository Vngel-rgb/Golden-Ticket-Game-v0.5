## Multiplayer response systems

The three simultaneous events and Exploding Candy defense now use a persisted room event:

- Slugworth Sizzler
- Invisible Gumdrop
- Ficklegruber’s Juicy Bar
- Exploding Candy defender reaction

Each eligible player now answers on their own device. Responses are stored in that
player's persisted private state, survive reconnects, and cannot be submitted twice
from the same state revision. The room remains outside the normal turn phase until
all required responses arrive. Final resolution is claimed with a conditional room
update, preventing two clients from resolving the same event.

No new Supabase tables or SQL migration are required for this pass.
